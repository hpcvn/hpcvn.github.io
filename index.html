About

A research lab at Moreh Vietnam

The Efficient Computing Research Lab is a research group at Moreh Vietnam in Hanoi. Since 2023 it has studied how to run AI workloads in less time and memory on GPUs and other accelerators.

We pick open problems that show up when large models meet real hardware, measure them carefully on NVIDIA and AMD GPUs, and publish what we learn. We work with researchers at Seoul National University and VNU University of Engineering and Technology.

Research

Three problem areas

Each area pairs a systems question with measurements on real GPUs.

LLM serving

How to serve large models to many users with less memory and shorter waits. We study how serving engines schedule requests, where the KV cache and the experts of mixture-of-experts models live in memory, and how work is split across GPUs. New ideas are tested in open-source engines such as vLLM and SGLang.

  • KV cache
  • MoE
  • prefill / decode
  • TP · DP · EP

Low-precision computing

Running models with fp8 and fp4 weights and KV cache while keeping their answers accurate. We look at where a model can drop to lower precision and where it has to stay higher, and at kernels that switch precision when memory or latency is tight.

  • fp8
  • fp4
  • quantization
  • mixed precision

Fast and correct GPU kernels

A new kernel, whether written by hand, by a compiler or by an AI model, is only useful if it is both faster and right. We build latency predictors that let autotuning and kernel search need fewer test runs, and error checks that catch kernels whose results are slightly wrong, on NVIDIA and AMD GPUs.

  • Triton
  • autotuning
  • latency prediction
  • error detection

Hardware we use: NVIDIA V100, A100 and H100; AMD Instinct MI250.

Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering, Seoul National University. B.S. in Computer Science, VNU University of Engineering and Technology.

His research focuses on GPU optimization and efficient systems for large language model training and inference, including kernel tuning, low-precision computing and model serving. He is also committed to education, bringing emerging topics in parallel and AI computing into university courses and hands-on workshops, and to building a high-performance computing community in Vietnam.
